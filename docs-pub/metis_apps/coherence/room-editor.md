# Coherence: Setting up your event's room

Every Coherence event has a **room**: the place your conversation's participants arrive
at, give their consent, talk, and leave from. The room's words and pictures belong to the
event, and you set them in the **room editor**.

> This page assumes you can find your event in the app (see
> [Events & conversations](events-and-conversations.md)) and that you can edit it. If you
> can see the event but not the section below, you cannot edit the event; ask whoever
> manages it.

---

## 1. Opening the editor

Open your event's page. Its **Janus Experience** section shows how far the room is from
ready, and a **Set up the room** button. The button opens the editor.

The section shows only to people who can edit the event, and only on an event that has a
room. Every event created in Coherence gets one. If yours has none, platform staff can
create it.

## 2. Getting ready

At the top of the section, and again at the top of the editor, **Getting ready — n of …
done** lists what the room still needs before you share it:

- **Welcome message**: what people read in the entry lounge before they go in.
- **Consent text**: what people agree to before the conversation is recorded.
- **Closing message**: what people read in the exit lounge afterwards.
- **At least one question** for the conversation.

Each unfinished item is a link to the place in the editor where you set it. When
everything is done the list turns into **Ready — share the link**.

## 3. Sharing the link

When the room is ready, the list shows, for each of the event's conversation journeys, the
journey's link to share and a **Copy link** button, plus **Schedule a conversation**, which
opens that journey's conversation board.

## 4. The spaces

The editor shows the room one space at a time, in the order participants go through it.
Pick a space from the strip at the top.

| Space | What you can set |
|---|---|
| **Entry lounge** | The welcome message, the background (an image you upload or a YouTube link), the lounge's **sound**, and the **arrival sound** |
| **Consent** | The consent text |
| **Starting recording** | The sound played as the recording starts |
| **Conversation** | Nothing to set: the event's questions are listed here, and you edit them on each journey's page |
| **Ending conversation** | Nothing in this version |
| **Exit lounge** | The closing message, the background, the sound played on leaving, and the **goodbye address**: the web page people are sent to when they leave |

Every setting is either **Use inherited**, which shows what the room would get from its
scene, or **Custom**, your own value for this room. **Save** saves the space you are on.

### The preview

Beside the settings (below them on a narrow screen) the editor shows the space as
participants see it, with made-up people, drawn by the room itself. It follows the space
and audience you pick. As you type, it shows your change within a moment, marked **Not
saved yet**: nothing reaches the real room until you click **Save**. A value that is not
finished yet (half a web address) is not shown; the preview keeps the last good picture
and says why. Sounds, uploads and removals save when you click them, and the preview
plays no sound. Where the preview is not available yet, the editor says so.

The bar above the spaces holds the room's **name** (Rename) and its **scene**: a shared
look several rooms can wear. **Reset all customisations**, at the foot of the editor,
removes every custom setting, uploaded background and chosen sound from the room and keeps
the scene. Your organisation's sounds stay.

### Sounds

A room can play a short sound at a few moments:

- **Entering the entry lounge**, **the recording starting**, and **entering the exit
  lounge** (leaving): heard by the person who gets there.
- **Someone arriving** (the **Arrival sound**, set on the entry lounge): heard by
  everyone already in the room.

Each sound setting, under **Custom**, lets you:

- pick a sound already uploaded anywhere in your organisation (listed by file name),
- choose **No sound**, so nothing plays even where the scene has a sound, or
- upload a new one: MP3, OGG, M4A or WAV. The file is checked for what it really is,
  whatever its name says. An upload is added to your organisation's sounds, so every
  other room can pick it too.

Next to each sound is its **Sound volume**: *Quiet*, *Medium*, *Loud* or *Full*. Left
unset, it plays at *Full*. **Use inherited** plays the scene's sound for that moment, if
it has one. Letting go of a sound in one room never removes it from the others.

## 5. When a change reaches participants

A saved change reaches the room at its **next conversation**. A conversation already under
way keeps what it started with.

## 6. Where the room's words used to be

Before the room editor, the welcome, consent, closing and goodbye address, and the lounge
background, were set on each conversation journey's page. Those settings are now the
room's, and the journey page no longer has them. When the room editor arrived, each event's
words and background were copied onto its room, so participants saw the same thing.

Where an event's journeys showed different words, nothing was copied for that event: its
room shows those settings as not done, and you set them in the editor.

## Common gotchas

- **"I don't see the Janus Experience section"**: you cannot edit this event, or it is not
  a Coherence event, or it has no room.
- **"I changed the welcome but a participant still saw the old one"**: they were already
  in a conversation; the change shows at the next one.
- **"Where did the welcome text on the journey page go?"**: it is the room's now; see §6.
